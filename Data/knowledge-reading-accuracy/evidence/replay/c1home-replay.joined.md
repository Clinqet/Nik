# Glossary Of Terms

## Additional Living Expense

Coverage applicable when an insured's dwelling is damaged by an insured peril to such an extent that one cannot live in it until repaired. This portion of the insurance policy pays the extra amount it costs to live elsewhere until repairs are made, such as the cost of living in a hotel or rent.

## All Risk Coverage

Provides coverage against the loss caused by all perils except those which are specifically excluded by the terms of the policy.

## By-law Endorsement

An endorsement explaining how a particular insurance company deals with a claim which is affected by a local by-law. This coverage will apply to any increased cost of construction due to a change in building code requirements or the loss of value to undamaged parts of the building. It can also apply to the cost of demolition to an undamaged portion of the building including the associated cost for debris and removal.

## Comprehensive Form

All risk coverage for both building and contents, except for specific exclusions found in the policy wordings.

## Contents Insurance (Personal Property)

This portion is the amount of coverage for all personal contents on the policy. This coverage is for the personal property owned, used or worn by the insured which is usual and normal to the ownership and maintenance of the property.

## Deductible

An agreed, specified sum to be deducted from the amount of loss and assumed by the insured. This is the amount that the insured is required to pay before the coverage on a policy will respond.

## Dwelling Coverage

This applies to your home and "attached structures" such as a garage or carport. Permanently installed outdoor equipment on the premises, such as a swimming pool and the equipment attached to it, is usually included. Building materials for use in construction, alteration or repair of the insured dwelling or related structures on the premises are usually covered too - if they are on the site or adjacent to it. This coverage also applies to outdoor trees, shrubs and plants for certain perils.

## Effective Date

The date on which an insurance policy or bond goes into effect, and from which protection is furnished.

## Expiry

End of the policy period.

## Extended Coverage Insurance

Protection for the insured against loss or damage of his property caused by windstorm, hail, smoke, explosion, riot, riot attending a strike, civil commotion, impact of vehicle and aircraft. This is provided in conjunction with the fire insurance policy.

## Fire Insurance

Coverage for losses from fire and lightning and also the resultant damage caused by smoke and water. Usually supplemented by Extended Coverage Insurance.

## Guaranteed Replacement Cost

Coverage that pays for replacement without reduction for depreciation. A guaranteed replacement cost endorsement covers any shortfall in the event that the replacement cost of a building has been underestimated.
<!-- PageBreak -->
## Hail Insurance

Insurance of property against damage which occurs by hailstorm. Usually refers to growing crops but coverage is also provided under other policies such as
for automobiles, buildings and their contents. In some cases, a separate deductible for this peril may apply.


## Insured

The entity (individual or otherwise) whose risk of financial loss from an insured peril is protected by the insurance policy.


## Outbuilding or Detached Private Structures

Coverage for structures or buildings on an insured's premises that are separated from the dwelling by a clear space. Policies generally include a limit for this
coverage, with the option to purchase increased values if necessary.


## Overland Water

An additional coverage that can be purchased to protect insureds from losses due to fresh water entering the property. See policy wordings for carrier
specific details.

Peril

An event which may cause a loss to occur.


## Personal Liability

A form of liability insurance for individuals which insures the policyholder in the event they have become liable to pay money for damage or injury they have
caused to others. This form does not include automobile liability, but does cover almost every activity of the policyholder except those which arise from the
operations of a business.


## Protected

A property located in an area that is regularly serviced by a fire department and has hydrant access within 300 meters of the dwelling/ building.


## Rental Value Insurance (Rental Income)

Insurance which reimburses the owner/occupant of a building for the cost of renting other premises if his building is rendered unusable by some peril
insured against.


## Replacement Cost

The cost to replace, repair or rebuild the lost or damaged property without deduction for depreciation.


## Sewer Back-Up (Water Damage Extension)

This endorsement will cover the sudden and accidental escape of water from a sewer, sump pump, eaves troughs, downspouts, septic tank and drains. A
separate deductible may apply.


## Single Limit of Insurance

This limit is the combination of sections A (building/ dwelling), B (Other structures), C (Personal Property), and D (loss of use/ additional living expenses) on a
personal property policy. The limits of these sections are added together to create a single limit of insurance, designed to prevent shortfalls in the amount in
any one section.


## Voluntary Medical

This coverage can be used by an insured for payment of medical expenses of an individual, when they are not legally liable for the damages, but voluntarily
wish to compensate the party after they accidentally injure themselves. It will also cover injury sustained by resident employees while working for you.


## Voluntary Property

This coverage can be used by an insured for payment of accidental physical damage to the property of others, when they are not legally liable for the
damages, but voluntarily wish to compensate the party.


## Water

A peril generally included in the basic policy wording of an insurance policy. Carrier specific wordings should be consulted for policy coverage, limits and
deductibles, as they vary per carrier.

Note: The information above represents generic insurance industry definitions only. Please review your policy for the exact definitions and your specific coverage
information.
