# Clinket — outreach email (copy, personalize, send)

**How to use this:** swap the `[...]` parts, keep it short, and always add one personal line
at the top for the person you're writing to ("Loved your talk at X", "John suggested I reach
out"). Attach: the Executive Summary + the Deck. Send the full Investor Overview only when
they ask for more — a lighter first email gets opened; the heavy document is the follow-up.

**Should the future growth be in the email?** One line of it, yes — it's the hook that makes
people curious. But not the details: the email's only job is to get the summary opened and a
call booked. The financial layer and the one-app-for-everything story land much harder in the
documents and in person.
