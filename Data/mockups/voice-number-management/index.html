Same design, three events — removed, changed and added (re-add after removal) share one responsive skeleton; a provider’s first-ever activation keeps its existing welcome email. All five channels (email · push · in-app · web · SignalR) fire from one dispatch. SMS is one app-setting (default on) that gates the text for all three events; +91 (India DLT) is always excluded in code, and when SMS is off every other channel still fires. A reason is shown to the provider on removed / changed; added is positive news so it carries no reason line (still captured in the audit).
This admin surface is kept simple and consistent with the existing pages — its only job is to be useful for your team. Look up by number / email / phone (same as WhatsApp Admin), then Change or Remove the number. Each destructive action is one dialog (that dialog is the confirmation).
