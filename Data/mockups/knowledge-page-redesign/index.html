§4 — Every important action asks first (8 dialogs)
Today only "Approve all remaining" and "Dismiss all from document" confirm; a stray tap on Approve, Update my service, Dismiss, or "Approve these 25" acts immediately. v4 guards all of them with the platform ConfirmationModal (web) / ConfirmDialog bottom sheet (mobile). "Save and approve" inside the editor stays unguarded — the editor itself is the deliberate step. Exact copy below; green confirm for adding, red for dismissing.
