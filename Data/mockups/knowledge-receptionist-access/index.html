What the receptionist may do with each file

One question with three answers, per file and per typed FAQ, in the AI Knowledge editors and at upload time — the mark it leaves on the row, the page when a business has no receptionist (nothing about it is drawn), and the moment one is switched on. Every state, web and phone. It replaces the single “Send relevant details to callers” switch of sheet M6 §1 and the upload line of knowledge-document-sharing v3; the Team search box is unchanged.

0 · What this sheet changes, and what it leaves alone

Today a file has two settings: whether the receptionist may send its details to a caller, and who on the team may find it. Nothing says whether the receptionist may use the file at all — so an internal document is read aloud to callers the moment it is Ready. This sheet gives that question a home, hides it from a business that has no receptionist, and shows it — with the saved answer — the day one is switched on.

8 · A business with no receptionist — nothing about it is drawn
