4 · Rules

  • "Comes to you" = every service that is not "at their place only" (the existing rule, D-104). "At their place only" keeps today's card exactly (D).
  • Inside = the customer is within the radius of one of that service's areas (the existing coverage rule). The area named is the one containing the customer; if several contain them, the nearest centre.
  • Outside every area: the area named is the one whose centre is nearest, and the distance is to that centre (owner decision — the same rule the leads matcher uses).
  • "Based in" is the business address's city; hidden when it is the same city as the served area (owner decision). A business with no address shows no "Based in".
  • The area's name is the area's city; when an area has no city, its own name.
  • Distance sort: "serves you here" counts as 0 and those come first, ordered by rating; then everything else by distance. Exact for every result the list can show — the index checks containment itself (verified on the sandbox index), nothing is guessed from a partial window.
  • No location from the customer (E): no "Serves" line at all — nothing is claimed without a point.
