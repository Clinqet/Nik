Minute packs — who can add minutes, and what everyone else sees

Approved by the owner 2026-09-25, in conversation. Governs the minute-pack slot under the AI Assistant on the provider web billing page, and the (currently hidden) "Top up minutes" card in the provider app's Plan & Billing screen. Today the web shows the pack picker to a provider who has no AI Assistant, and to one whose payment failed; the purchase is then refused. This sheet makes the page say what is true instead.

Frames 10–18 added 2026-09-26 for "settle first" (FOLLOWUPS-PLAN G1–G5, the owner's decisions 2 and 3 of 2026-09-26), and frames 2–3 updated to lead with Pay now. They draw the states the approved decision describes; the owner has not yet looked at these frames themselves (the register row says so).

One rule decides the state, and the server owns it: the same check the purchase uses (ResolveTopUpEligibilityAsync) is shared by the billing overview, which sends one answer — topUpAvailability: Available · SettlePaymentFirst · TrialEnding · NoAssistant. The web and the app never work it out from dates. Words: web keys in public/lang/*.json (en-US, es-US, fr-CA, gu-IN, hi-IN); app keys in src/Locales/*.json (en, es, fr, gu, hi). "Update card" reuses the AI card's existing key and opens the same add-card sheet.
