Checkout price breakdown — customer web + phone

Every checkout (web quick book, web cart checkout, web cart booking pop-up; phone quick book, phone cart, phone checkout) shows the price the server will book, line by line, before the customer confirms. The app never adds anything up itself: every figure comes from POST /api/v1/bookings/price-preview, which prices the booking with the same code that creates it. Drawn 2026-09-24 for Q-1 of the search-topology Phase 3 audit.

The rule. The customer sees what the booking will cost, the way every shop checkout shows it: the services, then any discount, then the visit fee, then tax, then the total. The total is exactly what is booked. A travel fee depends on distance and is set by the provider when they confirm, so it is named but never added into the total.

A guest sees each service's price but no summary: the price is asked for only once they are signed in, and Confirm opens sign-in as it always has — after which the summary loads by itself. (The phone never asks while signed out: an unauthorised call there would clear the stored guest cart.)
A refusal is the server's already-translated sentence (a service the provider no longer lists, an offer that does not apply on the chosen day, a provider who is not taking online bookings). The screen never writes its own version of it.

  • The preview is asked for when the checkout opens, and again when its services, offers or date change; an older answer that arrives late is ignored.
  • Confirm stays disabled while the price is loading, could not be got, or was refused.
  • A checkout covering several providers shows one summary per provider — each is its own booking, priced on its own.
  • Money is formatted in the preview's currency with the app's existing money formatter.
  • The confirmation screen after booking shows the booked total from the booking response.
