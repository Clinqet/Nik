2 · Rules

  • Same action as every other list card: the booking's services are added to the cart and the cart opens (today's list behaviour after a completed booking). One button, one behaviour, across the list.
  • Shown for: Completed (today) and "The provider didn't come" (new). Never for Cancelled, declined or a customer no-show on the LIST (the detail page keeps its own rules unchanged).
  • A service that is gone or a provider that stopped taking bookings: the existing failure toast, nothing added.
