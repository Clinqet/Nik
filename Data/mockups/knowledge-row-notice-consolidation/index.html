4 · The rules this sheet is asking to fix

  • Nothing outside a box. If a row has something to SAY about a document, it says it in a notice. The only lines that stay plain are the file’s own facts (pages, parts, size, date) and the pictures line — those are not messages, they are the row describing itself.
  • One action, and only when the message asks for one. “No services to suggest” is an outcome, not a problem, so it carries no link. Run again still exists, in the row’s menu, where every other whole-document action lives. A failed run, a stopped file and an unreadable replacement DO ask for something, and those keep their action — inside the box.
  • Lead with the short truth. Three sentences of grey text do not get read. A bold lead line carries the outcome; one sentence underneath carries the reassurance.
  • Tone by meaning, not by loudness. Quiet = an outcome needing nothing from the provider. Blue = something waiting for them. Amber = worth their eye. Red = it did not work.
  • No technical word anywhere a provider can read (§0.20): no passage, chunk, index, parse, extraction or payload. This sheet says file, page, part, priced line, services, callers.
