Chat messages on WhatsApp — what the customer and the provider see

Every state, both directions, web and phone. Approved by the owner on 20 September 2026, including the in-thread delivery line (frames 5h, 5i). Revised the same day at the owner's request: a real "Open chat" button on the first message of a burst (1a, 1g, 3a), the long-message case sent as two messages so the button is always there (1h), real tick states in the app frames (5h, 5i) and a tick legend (5j). Words shown in existing frames already exist in the apps; words marked NEW are proposed and will be translated into all five languages before they ship.

What changes for people: when someone is chatting with us on WhatsApp, their reply arrives on WhatsApp and in the app, and they no longer also get an email for the same message. The email is sent only if WhatsApp could not deliver. Inside the app the sender now sees a delivered tick when WhatsApp confirms the copy reached the phone, and a quiet line under the message if the WhatsApp copy could not be delivered. Tapping a WhatsApp alert opens the exact conversation.

1 · The customer's phone — a reply arrives on WhatsApp

Applies when the customer wrote to us on WhatsApp within the last 24 hours. The full message arrives as a normal WhatsApp message from Clinket. The sender's name is always shown. The first message of a fresh burst carries a real "Open chat" button and a short footer; later messages in the same burst are plain, so a live back-and-forth is never cluttered.

4 · The email — sent only when WhatsApp could not deliver

The email is the one that exists today, unchanged. It goes out if WhatsApp reports a failure within 6 hours of the message, or if WhatsApp has still not confirmed delivery after 2 hours. It is never sent when the person has already read the message in the app, muted or hidden the conversation, blocked the sender, or when the message was removed by Clinket support.

5 · Inside the app — the conversation (unchanged words, every state)

The thread looks as today, plus two things for the sender: a delivered tick when WhatsApp confirms the copy reached the phone, and a quiet line if the WhatsApp copy could not be delivered (5h, 5i). Provider web, provider phone app, customer web and customer phone app.

5j · the sender's ticks — legend (existing states plus one)
Sent (existing: single grey tick)
Delivered on WhatsApp (NEW: double grey tick)
Read (existing: double green tick — read in the app or on WhatsApp)
Could not send (existing: the message never left the app)
The ticks never go backwards: sent → delivered → read. A late "delivered" after "read" changes nothing. The delivered tick is set only when the words themselves travelled on WhatsApp (a live-chat copy); the after-24-hours notice never sets it — the person got a notice, not the message. Labels for screen readers: existing inbox.statusSent / inbox.statusRead / inbox.statusFailed (and the messages.* twins), plus NEW inbox.statusDelivered / messages.statusDelivered — "Delivered on WhatsApp". Phone apps use the same four-then-five states in their existing tick component.

8 · What the sender is told when a WhatsApp copy fails

No separate alert, no pop-up, no "try again". The outcome appears as a quiet grey line under the sender's own message (frames 5h, 5i): "Couldn't reach {name} on WhatsApp — sent by email instead." or "… — they'll see it here in Clinket." The message itself is in the conversation and the other person had it in the app and by push. Clinket support is alerted behind the scenes. The existing "please try again" alert stays for booking and quote texts and emails, where that advice is right.
