AI Assistant page — density, the disconnect action, and the ring you choose

Eight changes the owner asked for after testing the live page on 16 September. Nothing here invents a feature: every panel already exists, and this sheet only says where it lives, when it is open, and what it is allowed to claim.

Amendment, approved with the sheet (PLAN D-17): the ring options ship with the SECONDS ONLY. The "about two rings" line under each option in §04 and §05 is drawn here and was dropped before build — a ring is a carrier cadence, not a constant, so the count was a guess printed as a fact. The frames below are otherwise as built.

Owner changes after build, 2026-09-17 (PLAN D-31 to D-34) — these override the frames below: the settings form is NOT two columns — only Phone setup and Languages share a row, and every other section is a full-width row in its original order (voice as two columns of cards, recording under hours, data & retention and help improve stacked). Pause assistant and Private mode sit in the LEFT column under who answers. The knowledge line sits after the settings, just above Cancel assistant, as before the number cards (D-37). Disconnect is a red outline button directly under the number card's actions with one line BELOW it, before "How it works" (D-35, D-38) — not a link on its header. The phone adds pull-to-refresh and haptic feedback.

Amended again 2026-09-17 by voice-own-number-honest-status (PLAN D-42 to D-49), which overrides these frames on: the default ring time is 10 seconds, not 15 (D-42). After voicemail picks up first, the step-3 ring choice locks that time and every longer one, recommends the next shorter one, and holds code 2 until a shorter time is saved (D-43, D-44). The connected card states the time they chose, with the test call's stopwatch as its own line (D-45).

  • “Who answers your calls” moves up out of the settings section and sits beside the two numbers it describes. It is the legend for the right column, and it was the one panel a provider needed while looking at both numbers. It still renders only when the provider keeps their own number.

06 · “Not available here” — two different reasons, two different sentences

A provider in India is told their region is not supported. That is not what happened: the region is switched on, and the stamp simply has no verification number yet. One sentence cannot honestly serve both.

Region is offunchanged
Connecting your own number isn't available in your region yet.

Your Clinket number works everywhere as normal.

Region on, number missingnew
We're finishing the set-up for your country.

Your assistant works as usual in the meantime. Connecting your own number switches on here as soon as it's ready.

One thing I cannot decide for you. Canada and the United States are being set to +1 844 925 4651 as instructed. India runs on Plivo, which refuses to place a call from a number the account does not own — so a made-up Indian number would not open the flow, it would fail at the carrier on every check. Send me any Plivo number on our India account and India switches on in one line.

09 · What this sheet does NOT change

  • No new data, no new endpoint, no extra request on any screen. The ring picker uses the ring-seconds route that the adjust dialog already calls.
  • The verification rules are untouched — what a check proves, what a disconnect proves, and every failure's own fix stay exactly as approved and built.
  • The invitation state, the landline route, the ask-us arrangement and the carrier-untested banner keep their current shapes and words.
  • Nothing is hidden behind a screen width. Every control on a laptop is on the phone, and the two-column grids collapse to one column below 1024 with no loss.
