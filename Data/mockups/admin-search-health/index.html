What this design promises
  • An empty index is a content observation, not proof of failure. Live empty indexes remain clearly visible.
  • A genuine degraded or unhealthy status always has a reason, affected scope, impact and next step.
  • Failed monitoring requests show Unknown. Old results remain labelled with their original check time.
  • The server owns the status and diagnostic wording. The browser owns request/loading/access messages.
  • Checks cover index reachability and dictionary readiness. They do not certify indexing completeness or search relevance.

All states are reviewable above, including empty, loading, failure, denied access, throttling, offline and recovery. Actions inside these isolated frames are labelled previews.
