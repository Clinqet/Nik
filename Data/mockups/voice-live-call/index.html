How this satisfies the cost & performance requirement

  • Targeted streaming only. The Live Call screen invokes WatchVoiceLive on mount (and re-asserts on every SignalR reconnect — groups don’t auto-rejoin) and UnwatchVoiceLive on unmount. A provider who isn’t on this screen receives zero transcript fan-out — identical to web. The 20s poll is dropped in favour of push.
  • “Call in progress” push. The existing VoiceLiveCallStarted notification deep-links straight here (new route added to notificationNavigation.ts); an in-app banner offers one-tap entry while foregrounded.
  • Join from the app = best-practice VoIP. react-native-webrtc + Telnyx’s official RN SDK with CallKit/ConnectionService for correct audio focus & routing, behind a LiveCallTransport seam — Telnyx now (Canada), Plivo later (India), cleanly separate.
  • Graceful fallback. If the native SDK/region can’t do app-audio, “Join from the app” hides and “Ring my phone” remains — the screen never dead-ends.
  • Summary & recording are async — and we say so. On end the screen shows “Call ended · preparing your summary” (never a dead “View summary”). It unlocks the instant CallSummaryReady arrives over SignalR (bounded poll fallback if the push is missed); until then the provider can leave and gets the push. The recording is copied by a separate message and can land later — the player shows “preparing… / will be available shortly” and self-retries a fresh SAS rather than erroring. The history list & needs-review badge also refresh in place when CallSummaryReady fires (matching web).
