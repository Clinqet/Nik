Rules the screen followsNumbered markers in the text point to the cards below; green = one of your documents, blue = one of your services. The grey quote under each card is the exact text the answer used — already on screen, costs nothing. Pictures shown are only pictures the assistant actually found in the cited documents; tapping opens the full-size viewer used on the Knowledge page. "Page 2" appears only for files that really have pages (PDF, slides, scans) — never for Word/Excel/web files.
Your cost question is solved better than a download linkYou asked whether there is a cheaper alternative to letting people open the file. There is, and we already have it: when a PDF or a scan is processed we keep each page as text. So the first action is "Show page 4" — it opens the whole page, reads a few kilobytes, renders in the same panel on web and phone, and needs no PDF viewer and no file download at all. "Open the original file" stays as a second, deliberate action for anyone who wants the real document. Word, Excel and web files are not stored page by page and have no real page number, so they show the section instead.
Why sources are built by the platform, not the assistantThe assistant may only refer to what the search returned; the cards are generated from those results. A made-up reference simply has nothing to attach to, so it cannot appear. This is also the guardrail against ever showing another business's material: every result is checked against the signed-in business before it reaches the assistant.
Page numbers come freeEvery searchable piece of text already stores the page it came from (verified in code: the knowledge index has a pageNumber field, real for PDFs, scans and slide decks). No index change and no re-processing is needed — only a display rule: the page is shown for files that really have pages (PDF, photos, PowerPoint = "Slide N") and hidden for Word, Excel, web and text files, where the stored value is a placeholder.
Decision 4 — follow-up questionsRecommended yes (a short conversation memory, few turns, kept briefly). Alternative: every question stands alone (cheapest, less natural).
