Why “Service suggestions” earned a tab and pictures do not

Suggestions are a queue of work that arrives on its own: they pile up, they carry a pending count, they are approved or dismissed in a batch, and the provider genuinely does not care which file each came from. A tab is exactly right for that.

Pictures are the opposite. They are part of a file, they arrive already decided, there is no queue and no batch, and the one control that governs them — “Send relevant details to callers” — belongs to that file. Take a picture away from its document and you have to reintroduce the document on every tile.

So: Option A, inside the document, hidden until asked for. If you would rather have the tab, say so and I will build B instead — it is the same tiles and the same rules either way.

8 The rendering contract

What each part draws and from which field. Both apps follow this table, so “mobile mirrors web” stays a rule a reviewer can check rather than a hope.

PartSourceRule
Everything hereimagesEnabled Off ⇒ no line, no panel, and no picture request is ever made.
The closed lineimageCount, sendableImageCount Drawn only when imageCount > 0. Never a picture URL — this list re-reads itself every few seconds while anything is processing.
Tile picturethumbnailUrl Absent ⇒ grey tile, plus “Processing…” while the thumbnail is still being made. A grid never pulls full-size pictures — forty of them is tens of megabytes.
Viewer picturegetOriginalSrc(image) → url The app's existing shared helper. These have an original and a thumbnail only — no medium — so the viewer takes the original, one at a time.
Ticksendable Brand green when on. Disabled and dimmed while the file's own switch is off.
Captioncaption Viewer only. Missing ⇒ the line is left out, never replaced with a placeholder sentence.

9 The two rules you asked me to confirm

1 · A picture follows its document, then the provider decidesas you described

A picture can be sent only when the file's “Send relevant details to callers” switch is on and that picture is ticked. Switch off ⇒ nothing from the file goes out, pictures included — and the ticks are kept exactly as they were, ready for when it goes back on.

Where the ticks start: a photo, a product shot or a diagram starts ticked; a logo, decoration, or a screenshot of text starts unticked — the rule you approved in the design (§13.2). A starting point only; every one can be changed. A picture Clinket AI could not describe also starts unticked, because it could not be spoken about on a call.

2 · Delete yes, add noas you described

Delete removes the picture from Clinket AI and from storage, and it stays deleted when the file is read again later. Without that, re-processing a file would quietly bring back every picture the provider had removed.

No add. This panel describes what is inside the provider's own file and every picture in it can be traced to a page of that file. Uploading one would break that, and pictures a provider chooses already have a home — the portfolio.
