> `CLAUDE.md` §0.20 (and its three twins) makes this folder the **only** home for a mockup sheet. `C:\Nik\mockups\`
> is gone and must never be recreated; a sheet inside a repo is forbidden and worse than scratch, because it is one
> `git add .` from being committed — and two already were.
>
> ‼️ **This file is an INDEX of what is present, not a record of approval.** §0.20 requires each programme to keep
> its own **mockup register** — sheet · path · approval date · what it governs · what it supersedes — in that
> programme's authority document, and a row goes in the day the owner says yes. The only such register today is
> `Data\provider-ai-search\PLAN.md` §0. Every row below whose *Register* column says **none** is a sheet whose
> approval status lives nowhere: findable now, but still owed a register row by whichever programme owns it.
>
> Titles are read from each sheet's own `<title>`; `—` means the sheet declares none. Nothing here is inferred:
> no dates, no approvals and no ownership have been guessed.
