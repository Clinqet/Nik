Pause my listing

A business can step out of Clinket search, suggestions, city pages and quote matching for a while and come back with one tap. Nothing else changes: their own Clinket page link keeps working, bookings through that link keep landing, their phone receptionist keeps answering, and Ask Clinket keeps working for the team. This sheet draws the control, what it says, and every state it can be in, on the provider web app and the provider phone app. Search-side, the backend already honours the pause today (the isListed field is written by both indexers and required by every customer-facing filter); only the control that sets it is new.

Where it lives, and why. The control is a third card on the Business details page, directly under “Show my business in Clinket search”, because the three switches on that page answer three sibling questions: appear in search or not · temporarily closed or open · take online bookings or calls only. Unlike its two siblings it saves the moment you confirm, through its own request, not with the page's “Save changes” button: a provider leaving on holiday must not have to re-save their whole profile, and a provider coming back must not find the pause still on because a form was left unsaved. While paused, an amber banner sits at the top of every dashboard page (web) and of the home tab (phone), with the Resume action on it, so a forgotten pause is impossible to miss on the next sign-in.
What pausing does not do (and the words on the screen say so): it does not touch existing bookings, leads or conversations; it does not hide the business's own Clinket page from anyone who has its link; it does not affect Google or other search engines; it does not stop the phone receptionist; it does not stop the team's own Ask Clinket. The Insights scores are frozen while paused, never lowered. Resume takes effect within a couple of minutes as search catches up (the same delay any profile change has today).

0 · “Promote my business on Clinket” — the existing card, reworded owner decision 2026-09-21

The switch that decides whether a business is in the customer marketplace keeps its meaning and its default (ON) but loses the flat title “Show my business in Clinket search”. It now says what it does for the provider: free promotion and leads. Switching it OFF asks once, in plain words, and saves immediately through the same listing request as the pause switch. OFF removes the business from every customer index (it is not a hidden mark); ON puts it back within a couple of minutes. Search engines and the business's own page link are never affected.

What the server does when the switch is confirmed. One request (PUT api/v1/business/profile/listing with { isListed }), permission business.profile.update, refused when the business is not Active or not in the marketplace. It writes isListed and listingPausedAt (set on pause, cleared on resume) on the profile. That profile write is the change-feed event both search indexers already react to, so the business leaves (or re-enters) search, suggestions, city pages and quote matching on its own, in the same couple of minutes any profile change takes. The Insights scoring pause already reads the field. No other service changes. listingPausedAt is the one new stored fact and needs the owner's §0.7 yes before it is built.
Deliberately not drawn, deliberately not built now. A “resume automatically on this date” option. It needs a scheduled job and a second stored date; the banner already makes a forgotten pause impossible to miss. It is recorded in the plan as a later addition, not a gap in this sheet.
