DECISION 3

Wording of the pill

Rendered at real size, next to the Ready pill they will sit beside. Plain words, no product jargon.

AReadyDetails can be sentNot sharedRecommended. Says who and what. Pairs naturally with the switch label "Send relevant details to callers". Widest of the four.
BReadyCan be sentNot sentShort. But "Not sent" reads like a delivery failure on a page that also processes documents.
CReadySendableInternalShortest pair. "Sendable" is product-speak; "Internal" is good and clear.
DReadySharedInternal onlyCompact and honest. "Shared" alone could be read as shared with staff.
