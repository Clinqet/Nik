AI Knowledge — "Send details to callers" When a caller asks for something the receptionist found in the provider's material, it can now send them just those details — by WhatsApp or email, the caller's choice — as a clean one-page "Information from {Business}" PDF (and, for email, the same details in the body). Never the whole file. No AI-written text. One platform setting switches the whole feature on; one switch per document (on by default) lets the provider keep internal material out. SMS never. This page is the approval gate. Nothing is built.

1 · The one provider control — in the document editor, ON by default

A single switch row in the existing Document-details editor (web) / Edit-details card (mobile). No new screen, no checkboxes. It saves on tap, like Pause/Private on the AI Voice Assistant page.

3 · What the caller receives — the details, not the file

The platform lays out the 1–3 pieces of material that answered the caller into a one-page PDF (the same engine as booking/invoice PDFs). WhatsApp carries that PDF with one fixed message; email carries the same details in the body plus the PDF. Shown for the live-demo case: "send me the inspection report for the 2016 D6".

Deterministic layout, zero AI text — and it reads whole. Header/footer are the invoice PDF's own composition: the provider's logo (small, top-left; falls back to the name if missing) and the Clinket logo in the footer — no watermark (recommended off: a logo behind text hurts reading). A card never cuts a sentence or a row; a long record split across pieces is joined automatically (bounded at ~2 pages, then "… continues — the owner can send the rest"); label: value records become a two-column table; a sentence repeated between adjacent pieces is shown once; only the provider's own text is sendable (never the AI-written overview or image descriptions). Hindi/Gujarati material needs an embedded font (today's PDFs have none) — included.
Caps and timing. ≤3 pieces per send, ≤2 sends per call, ≤~2 pages; unknown refs ignored, none valid ⇒ refusal. The tool only validates and queues (under a second, on the call); the PDF is rendered and sent in the background — typically 3–10 s to WhatsApp/email. Before delivery is wired, sample PDFs rendered from the live index are sent to you for approval.

4 · What the caller hears

Caller: Can you send me that inspection report?

Receptionist: Of course — to your WhatsApp on this number, or by email?

Caller: WhatsApp.

(send_material_info with the refs of the 2 results that held the D6 record — nothing else)

Receptionist: Sending you the inspection details to your WhatsApp now — it'll be there in a moment. Anything else about the D6?

Caller: Text it to me.

Receptionist: I can send it to your WhatsApp on this number, or by email — which do you prefer?

(a text message is never offered for the owner's material)

Caller: Email me the cost sheet.

(that document is switched off — its results carry no ref)

Receptionist: I can't send that one out, but I can read you the key points now — or take your details so the owner sends it to you. Which would you prefer?

Cost and tokens, per send: the receptionist passes ~30 tokens (the refs); the platform reads ≤3 cards, renders one PDF, sends one WhatsApp utility message (≈ $0.01–0.04) or one email. Nothing is composed by the AI.
