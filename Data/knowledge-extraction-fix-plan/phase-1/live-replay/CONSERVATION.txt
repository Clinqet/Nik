THE ONE DIFFERENCE, AND IT IS A FIX. xlsx_merged_header_formats_hidden_regions, card 0, row 2:

  DEPLOYED  Service | Short hair | Long hair
  HEAD      Short hair | Long hair

The sheet's sub-header row is ["", "Short hair", "Long hair"]. The deployed H1 first-cell carry wrote the
PREVIOUS row's first cell into the empty one, inventing the word "Service" there (X-03). HEAD's HoldsAValue
guard leaves an empty cell empty. The word HEAD "loses" is a word the document never contained.

WHAT THIS REPLAY CANNOT PROVE, and why. The artefact's blocks were produced by the DEPLOYED PARSER and the
DEPLOYED judge, so replaying them cannot exercise:
  - the parser fixes on the PDF/image documents (they need a fresh Document Intelligence + vision pass), and
  - the three-reader adjudication itself (L-1, L-2, R-13), whose input is a fresh vision reading.
Those are proven by unit tests built FROM this live evidence (DocumentTranscriptionAdjudicationTests) and by
the office/text half below; the live end-to-end re-run needs the code on the stamp — see HANDOVER.md.
